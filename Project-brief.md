# Project brief

## Research Question

Which populated areas of Lagos City are most vulnerable to
natural flooding based on elevation, rainfall, proximity to
water bodies, and population density?

## Study Area

Lagos metropolitan urban area within Lagos State, Nigeria.

## Aim

To assess and map the relative spatial flood vulnerability of Lagos Metropolis by integrating population exposure, proximity to surface water, elevation, and rainfall intensity using GIS.

## Objectives

1. Map areas of low elevation.
2. Analyse rainfall patterns.
3. Calculate proximity to water bodies.
4. Map population exposure.
5. Produce a composite Flood Vulnerability Index.
6. Identify areas with the highest relative vulnerability.

## Problem statement

Lagos Metropolis is a densely populated coastal urban area exposed to flooding due to its low-lying terrain, extensive waterways, intense seasonal rainfall and continuing urban development. Flooding can result in significant impacts on people, infrastructure and communities. Identifying locations where environmental flood susceptibility overlaps with high population exposure can support spatial prioritisation and provide a basis for further flood-risk investigation.
