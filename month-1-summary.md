# Month 1 Summary

## Question

Which areas within Bashorun Ward are located within 300 metres of a health facility?

## Analysis Operation

I ran a **300-metre buffer analysis** around the health facilities in Bashorun Ward.

I chose the buffer operation because I wanted to identify the areas located within 300 metres of each health facility. This provides a simple way to examine the spatial coverage of health facilities within the study area.

## What I Expected and What I Got

I expected each health facility to have a buffer extending **300 metres from the facility**.

The result matched my expectation. The analysis produced circular buffer zones around the health facilities, with each buffer extending 300 metres from its corresponding facility.

The buffers also overlap in some locations because some health facilities are located relatively close to one another.

## What Surprised Me

One thing I noticed was the amount of overlap between some of the 300-metre buffers. This shows that some areas of Bashorun Ward are within 300 metres of more than one health facility, while other areas have less buffer coverage.

## What Data I Still Need

I still need additional data to carry out further analysis of the areas covered by the health facilities. In particular, I need population data and other relevant spatial datasets that can help determine how many people and settlements fall within the 300-metre health facility coverage areas.

## Conclusion

The 300-metre buffer analysis worked as expected and successfully identified areas within 300 metres of health facilities in Bashorun Ward. The result provides a useful basis for further spatial analysis of health facility coverage and population exposure.
