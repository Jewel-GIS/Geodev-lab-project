# Geodev-lab-project

## Research Question

Which populated areas of Bashorun Ward, Ibadan North LGA, Oyo State, are poorly served by both transportation infrastructure and health facilities?

### Week 4 — (Month 1 Result)

As part of the first month of analysis, a 300-metre buffer was created around health facilities in Bashorun Ward to identify areas located within 300 metres of a health facility.

The analysis produced 300-metre coverage zones around the health facilities.
This result provides an initial assessment of health-facility spatial coverage and forms part of the wider analysis of transportation and health-facility accessibility in Bashorun Ward.
